The Dimensional Weight Trap on Car Parts

Most car parts (bumpers, radiators, shock absorbers, brake rotors, and exhaust manifolds) arrive in oversized supplier packaging containing mostly empty space. Traditional freight forwarders (Aramex, DHL, FedEx) calculate shipping cost based on dimensional weight (L x W x H / 5000), often charging double or triple the actual weight of the part.
